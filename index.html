Deadlock at UN climate summit in Turkey

COP31 is deadlocked a day overtime — the draft deal has no mention of fossil fuels, a red line for the EU and Pacific island nations

The Daily

A Thanksgiving Dinner for $100? Yes, It’s Possible (and Delicious).

Yes — Andy Baraghani cooked Thanksgiving for four in New York City for $96.44 at Whole Foods

• Menu: braised chicken legs (no turkey in store), herby Persian rice with tahdig instead of stuffing, roast potatoes, honey-butter honeynut squash, chopped spoon salad, and apple tarte tatin

• He saved by cutting a pomegranate and a dip at checkout; a whole turkey elsewhere runs about $20 at last year's $1.34/lb

• His turkey rule: dry brine 12–36 hours uncovered for crisp skin — never wet brine

The rise of kidults

Adults with no kids now buy more toys and games than households with children — over 50% of US toy sales in the first half of the year

• The US toy industry is $30B+, half from grownups; global sales topped $120B, up 20% this year, driven by adults

• Lego led it with $700–$800 premium sets; the pandemic turned a niche into a habit that stuck

• Play is hardwired and cuts stress — start with 30 seconds to 5 minutes a day, unscheduled

Up First

The booming cash advance industry trapping America’s small businesses

Merchant cash advances cost a typical 25%–200% annualized and sit outside lending law — no license, no fee limits, daily debits from your bank account

• One Glendale promoter took $250,000 and owed almost $400,000, then was ‘carroted’ with a fake $750,000 line of credit at 9% that never came

• He ended over $1M in debt to friends and family, lost his house, and is suing for $5M+ instead of settling

• Banks reject ~20% of small-business loans — that gap is what feeds the industry

WSJ What's News

Is ‘Grand Theft Auto VI’ Gaming’s Last Blockbuster?

Probably, for this kind of game — journalist Chris Plant says the ever-bigger, hand-made blockbuster is on its way out; large games will survive (Nintendo, Call of Duty), but most studios should not take GTA-scale risk

• GTA VI took over a decade, thousands of people, and more money than a typical film, funded by earlier GTA games

• It is digital-only with no disc, and the PS6 reportedly drops the disc drive too

• No AI prompt will produce a GTA VII near this quality any time soon — hand-made is Rockstar's selling point
