Digital Foundry Direct Weekly

DF Direct Weekly #289: Should Zelda Use DLSS? Nintendo HDR Issues, Xbox FSR 4.1.2 Performance

The Vergecast

Torrenters are building epic media collections

• Getting in is a years-long status game: build ratio and reputation in easier trackers to reach elite movie communities; bounties pool credit for rarities — an unpublished Salinger story drew a ~6 TB bounty before a 1-of-25 print copy surfaced on eBay
