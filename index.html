Channels with Peter Kafka

Encore: Chuck Klosterman on Why Football Owns TV (and Why It Won’t Forever)

Answer: football was accidentally built perfectly for TV — stop-start downtime, constant resets and the central camera create tension and conversation; in 2023, 93 of the 100 most-watched U.S. broadcasts were NFL games

• Why it won't last: Klosterman says expansion cannot stop, rights costs will stall, a lockout follows, and a public that no longer plays will drop it like horse racing in the 1920s

• Madden and EA College Football taught a generation schematic football before Mahomes made video-game throws real

• Dozens of portfolio managers were fired or benched, leaving the last ~40 trading days of the year unusually risk-shy
